### How do I get started?
The best way to get started is to schedule a free 30-minute discovery call. During this call, we'll discuss your needs, answer your questions, and determine if we're a good fit for your project. Visit our website to schedule a call.

## Pricing & Billing

### What is your hourly rate?
Our standard consulting rate is $200 per hour. This applies to all consulting, implementation, and support work.

### Do you offer fixed-price projects?
Yes! For well-defined projects with clear deliverables, we offer fixed-price quotes. This gives you cost certainty and includes all planning, implementation, testing, and a 30-day support period.

### Do you offer retainer agreements?
We offer monthly retainer agreements starting at $1,500/month (8 hours). Retainers include priority scheduling, regular strategy calls, and discounted rates for additional work.

For hourly work, payment is due within 15 days of invoice. For projects, we require a 50% deposit to begin, with the balance due upon completion. Retainers are paid monthly in advance.

## AI Automation

### What types of automation can you implement?
We can automate many business processes including document processing, email management, data entry, report generation, customer support (chatbots), and custom workflows. If a task is repetitive and rule-based, it can likely be automated.

### Do I need technical expertise to use AI automation?
No! We design our solutions to be user-friendly and provide comprehensive training. Our goal is to make AI accessible to businesses without requiring technical expertise.

### How long does it take to implement an automation solution?
Implementation time varies by complexity. Simple automations can be completed in 1-2 weeks, while more complex integrations may take 6-12 weeks. We'll provide a timeline estimate during our discovery process.

### Will automation replace my employees?
Our automation solutions are designed to augment your team, not replace them. By automating repetitive tasks, your employees can focus on higher-value work that requires human judgment, creativity, and relationship building.
