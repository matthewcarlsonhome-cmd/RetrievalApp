# Matthew Carlson Consulting - Pricing & Engagement Options

## Consulting Rate

Our standard consulting rate is **$200 per hour**.

This rate applies to:
- Strategy consulting sessions
- Technical assessments
- Implementation work
- Training and workshops
- Ongoing support and maintenance

## Engagement Models

### Hourly Consulting
Best for: Short-term projects, specific problems, or ongoing advisory needs

- Billed in 15-minute increments
- No minimum commitment
- Flexible scheduling
- Detailed time tracking and invoicing

### Project-Based Pricing
Best for: Well-defined projects with clear deliverables

- Fixed price based on project scope
- Milestone-based payments
- Includes all planning, implementation, and testing
- Training and documentation included
- 30-day support period after completion

### Retainer Agreements
Best for: Ongoing support and continuous improvement

**Starter Retainer: $1,500/month**
- 8 hours of consulting per month
- Priority scheduling
- Email support
- Monthly strategy call

**Growth Retainer: $3,000/month**
- 16 hours of consulting per month
- Priority scheduling
- Email and phone support
- Weekly strategy calls
- Quarterly business reviews

**Enterprise Retainer: Custom pricing**
- Custom hour allocation
- Dedicated support team
- 24/7 emergency support
- On-site visits included
- Custom reporting and analytics
